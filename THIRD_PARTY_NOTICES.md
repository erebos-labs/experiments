These interactive implementations and their musical/visual presentations were prepared by Erebos Labs with AI assistance. The underlying mathematical ideas are established work; no claim of discovering synchronization theory or Braess’s paradox is made.

## Synchronization and audio

- Steven H. Strogatz (2000), *From Kuramoto to Crawford: exploring the onset of synchronization in populations of coupled oscillators*, Physica D 143, 1–20. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0167-2789(00)00094-4), [author-hosted reference](https://www.stevenstrogatz.com/articles/from-kuramoto-to-crawford-exploring-the-onset-of-synchronization-in-populations-of-coupled-oscillators-pdf). The displayed sinusoidal phase coupling is Kuramoto-style; our identical-clock graph model and musical mapping are explicitly a toy, rather than a reproduction of a field experiment.
- [W3C Web Audio specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio-1.1/) for audio-clock scheduling and OfflineAudioContext.
- [MIDI Association: Standard MIDI Files](https://midi.org/standard-midi-files) for the file-format reference. The MIDI writer and independent parser here are original implementations; specification text is not copied.

## Traffic

- Dietrich Braess (1968), *Über ein Paradoxon aus der Verkehrsplanung*, Unternehmensforschung 12, 258–268. English translation by Dietrich Braess, Anna Nagurney and Tina Wakolbinger (2005), *On a Paradox of Traffic Planning*, Transportation Science 39(4), 446–450, [author-hosted paper](https://homepage.rub.de/Dietrich.Braess/Paradox-BNW.pdf). The original affine-cost example is attributed; the coordinated control explicitly minimizes total/mean travel time rather than the maximum used-route time emphasized first in that paper.
- David Easley and Jon Kleinberg (2010), *Networks, Crowds, and Markets*, [chapter 8](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/kleinber/networks-book/networks-book-ch08.pdf). The 45-minute / x÷100 teaching example is rescaled to one flow unit per 1,000 modeled drivers and a continuous stationary-flow abstraction.
- Anna Nagurney and Ladimer S. Nagurney (2020), [*The Braess Paradox*](https://supernet.isenberg.umass.edu/articles/braess-encyc.pdf), for user equilibrium, system optimization, and demand dependence.

## Riffle shuffles and carries

The explorer is an educational implementation of established mathematics with
AI assistance. No new shuffle theorem, carries identity or randomness metric is
claimed.

- Dave Bayer and Persi Diaconis (1992), *Trailing the Dovetail Shuffle to its Lair*,
  The Annals of Applied Probability 2(2), 294–313,
  [primary paper](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/157/Papers/bayer_diaconis.pdf).
  The ideal GSR model, composition law, arrangement probability (Theorems 1/3),
  and 52-card reference TV table are attributed to this work. Its rising-sequence
  count corresponds here to one plus the inverse-permutation descent count.
- Persi Diaconis and Jason Fulman (2009), *Carries, Shuffling, and Symmetric Functions*,
  [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0902.0179v1), Section 2, for the bivariate
  Eulerian identity used for joint visible/inverse descent counts and conditioning.
- Persi Diaconis and Jason Fulman (2008), *Carries, Shuffling and An Amazing Matrix*,
  [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/0806.3583v1), for the carries/descent connection
  and constructive interpretation. Equality in distribution is distinguished
  from equality on the same untransformed random digit word.
- Mark Sellke, Jialu Shi and Jiamin Wang (2025), *Universality of Cutoff for Riffle Shuffling*,
  [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.22783v1), for the general-cut asymptotic
  question mentioned in the explorer. No asymptotic threshold is used as a
  finite 52-card guarantee; no correction claim about that paper is made.

The random-rotation example is an elementary illustration: uniform on the n
cyclic rotations has uniform top label and full-order TV `1 − n/n!`.
Descriptions use original words. No paper files, substantial source text or
third-party executable code are bundled. The existing copyright terms apply.
