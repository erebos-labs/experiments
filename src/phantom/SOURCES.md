# Primary sources

- [Wightman & Kistler author report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19910004658): virtual-source synthesis and headphone correction; selected text/captions consulted.
- [Wightman & Kistler 1999](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.426899): head/source movement; abstract only consulted.
- [Macpherson & Middlebrooks 2002](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.1471898): lateral cue weighting; author-uploaded primary text consulted. Lateral judgments do not establish polar-direction cue weights.
- [Langendijk & Bronkhorst 2002](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.1501901): spectral-band manipulations; author-uploaded primary text consulted. No universal single-notch height rule is claimed.
- [McAnally & Martin 2014](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2014.00210/full): primary text, including absolute-elevation discussion; task-specific head-motion results.
- [McLachlan et al. 2023](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2023.1027827/full): primary text; distinguish static spectra from dynamic spectral changes under small movements.
- [Kim et al. 2013](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.4799748): primary proceedings text; small motion-context experiment with acknowledged sensory/setup confounds.
- [Brimijoin & Akeroyd 2012](https://doi.org/10.1068/i7173sas): primary text; doubled-source-motion illusion geometry and spectral dependence. This model is not a perceptual replication.
- [Sridhar & Choueiri 2021](https://3d3a.princeton.edu/document/276): rigid-sphere equations 3–8 and finite-series method; no perceptual truncation threshold adopted.
- [Web Audio specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/): buffer and source lifecycle.

Calculations are generated from the included code. Published participant results are cited context, not observations made by this project. Sources retain their original rights.
