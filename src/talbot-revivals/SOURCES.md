# Sources and scope

- M. V. Berry and S. Klein (1996), *Integer, fractional and fractal Talbot effects*: https://michaelberryphysics.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/berry274.pdf . Modal, post-paraxial and finite-illumination framework; sections 2, 4 and finite-N excerpts of section 5. Their Talbot distance is d²/λ; the full-return distance here is 2d²/λ.
- M. Berry, I. Marzoli and W. Schleich (2001), *Quantum carpets, carpets of light*: https://michaelberryphysics.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/berry329.pdf . Conceptual context, selected opening pages and idealization limits; not a full-paper review.
- Guigay et al. (2004), *The partial Talbot effect and its use in measuring the coherence of synchrotron X-rays*: https://journals.iucr.org/s/issues/2004/06/00/ml5205/ml5205.pdf . Introduction and sections 2–3, equations 1–11; incoherent angular ensemble and Fourier-coherence damping. No reanalysis of measured data.

Authored finite numerical models and figures illustrate established ideas. No new discovery, full electromagnetic calculation, laboratory replication or universal grating theorem is claimed. Coherence and finite-illumination extensions remain separate calculations. No paper body, source figure, font binary or third-party runtime is included. Built with AI assistance.
