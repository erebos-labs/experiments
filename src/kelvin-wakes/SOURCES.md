# Primary sources

- Alexandre Darmon, Michael Benzaquen and Elie Raphaël, *Kelvin wake pattern at large Froude numbers*, Journal of Fluid Mechanics 738, R3 (2014). https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.6751 , https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2013.607 . Gaussian pressure spectrum, far-field residue integral and distinction between geometric caustic and amplitude maximum. The model follows arXiv v2, Eq. 2.8 and its Gaussian convention.
- Frédéric Moisy and Marc Rabaud, *Mach-like capillary-gravity wakes* (2014), Appendix A. https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.0422 . The appendix compares height, slope and curvature angle definitions. This explorer uses the pure-gravity model, not the paper’s capillary-gravity experiments.
- Marc Rabaud and Frédéric Moisy, *Ship wakes: Kelvin or Mach angle?* (2013). https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.2653 . Photographed wake features and the deep-water ray-angle construction. Its Gaussian size convention differs from the later paper; prefactors require consistent definitions.
- Frédéric Moisy and Marc Rabaud, *Scaling of far-field wake angle of non-axisymmetric pressure disturbance* (2014). https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.2049 . Scope caution: elongated disturbances add an aspect-ratio dependence.

The code, sampled outputs and diagrams are original educational implementations, not reproductions of published figures or measured ship datasets. Sources retain their rights. No source paper body is distributed.
