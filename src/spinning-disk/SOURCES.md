# Sources

- B. G. Thorne et al., “Air Drag Controls the Finite-Time Singularity of Euler's Disk” (2026), arXiv v1, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14520v1>. Source for the displayed two-loss scaling law, parameter controls, and late boundary-layer interpretation. This is a preprint. Its displayed boundary-layer thickness and following transition formula differ by a factor of 4 algebraically; this module documents and uses the direct, more conservative consequence of the displayed thickness convention.
- H. K. Moffatt, “Euler's disk and its finite-time singularity” (2000), <https://doi.org/10.1038/35009017>. Source for the original full-gap viscous finite-time model.
- L. Bildsten, “Dissipation for Euler's Disk and a Desktop Demonstration of Coalescing Neutron Stars” (2001 preprint; published 2002), <https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0102046>. Source for the finite-boundary-layer scaling.
- H. Caps et al., “Rolling/Slipping Motion of Euler's Disk” (2004), <https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0401278>. Source on rolling/slipping regimes and frictional dissipation.
- D. Ma, C. Liu, Z. Zhao, and H. Zhang, “Rolling friction and energy dissipation in a spinning disc” (2014), <https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2014.0191>. Source for six-degree-of-freedom measurements and rolling-friction scaling in its measured regime.
- A. V. Borisov, I. S. Mamaev, and Y. L. Karavaev, “On the loss of contact of the Euler disk” (2015), <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11071-014-1811-5>. Source for intermittent and terminal contact-loss measurements and base-plate dependence.
- B. W. Collins and S. J. Hogan, “An Experimental and Theoretical Study of Euler's Disk” (2026), <https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1711741>. Source for base-plate dependence and motion after the disk falls flat.

The equations, plots, synthetic fitting designs, prose, and code in this module are an original educational implementation. Links do not redistribute or sublicense the cited works.
